### `05 // combat doctrine`

- **Study the map** — understand the product, users and constraints before touching architecture
- **Choose the right build** — technology serves the mission, never the other way around
- **Think in systems** — APIs, data models, permissions, observability and failure paths belong together
- **Ship in checkpoints** — small releases, meaningful commits and visible progress reduce risk
- **Guard production** — security, validation, monitoring and recovery are part of the feature
- **Lead from the front** — unblock the team, review with context and own the final result
- **Use AI with intent** — accelerate research and implementation while keeping engineering judgment human
